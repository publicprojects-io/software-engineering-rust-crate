# Glossary

- **Bus factor:** Minimum number of people whose combined knowledge reaches a
  critical share of a system.
- **Change failure rate:** Percentage of deployments that cause a production
  failure requiring remediation.
- **Cycle time:** Elapsed time across coding, pickup, review, test, and deploy.
- **DORA metrics:** Deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure
  rate, and failed-deployment recovery time.
- **Error budget:** Downtime or unreliability allowed by an SLO over a period.
- **Escaped defect:** Defect discovered after the quality boundary, commonly in
  production.
- **Flow efficiency:** Active work time as a percentage of total elapsed time.
- **Flow load / WIP:** Items currently active or waiting in a value stream.
- **Flow time:** Elapsed time from an item's entry to delivery.
- **KLOC:** One thousand lines of code.
- **Little's law:** In a stable system, work in process equals arrival rate
  multiplied by flow time.
- **MTTA / MTTD / MTTR:** Mean time to acknowledge, detect, and resolve or
  recover from incidents.
- **Mutation kill rate:** Percentage of injected code mutations detected by the
  test suite.
- **Net revenue retention:** Ending revenue from a starting customer cohort as
  a percentage of its starting revenue, excluding new customers.
- **ROI:** Net benefit divided by investment cost.
- **SLO:** Service-level objective, a target value for a service indicator.
- **SPACE:** Satisfaction, Performance, Activity, Communication, and Efficiency;
  a multidimensional developer-productivity framework.
- **Takt time:** Available working time divided by customer demand.
